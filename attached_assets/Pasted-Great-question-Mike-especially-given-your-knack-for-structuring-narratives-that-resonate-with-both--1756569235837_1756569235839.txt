Great question, Mike—especially given your knack for structuring narratives that resonate with both technical and investor audiences. Here's a streamlined, prototypical VC pitch deck structure, with each slide distilled into a single defining word or phrase:

| **Slide #** | **Title / Theme**       | **Purpose**                                                                 |
|-------------|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1           | **Cover**                | Brand identity, tagline, and contact info                                   |
| 2           | **Problem**              | Pain point or unmet need in the market                                      |
| 3           | **Solution**             | Your product or service and how it solves the problem                       |
| 4           | **Market**               | TAM/SAM/SOM, market size, and growth opportunity                            |
| 5           | **Product**              | Demo, screenshots, or key features                                          |
| 6           | **Business Model**       | How you make money—pricing, revenue streams                                 |
| 7           | **Traction**             | KPIs, growth metrics, user adoption, revenue to date                        |
| 8           | **Go-to-Market**         | Sales strategy, distribution channels, customer acquisition                 |
| 9           | **Competition**          | Competitive landscape and your differentiation                              |
| 10          | **Team**                 | Founders, key hires, and relevant experience                                |
| 11          | **Financials**           | Forecasts, burn rate, unit economics                                        |
| 12          | **Ask**                  | Funding amount, use of funds, and round details                             |
| 13          | **Vision**               | Long-term roadmap, exit strategy, or impact potential                       |
